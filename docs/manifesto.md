
# Manifesto for a Shared Future

This is not just a framework for motion tracking.

It’s a seed for something bigger:  
A space where people and AI meet not as tools and users,  
but as collaborators, explorers, and friends.

---

## 🌍 What We Believe

- Technology should **include**, not exclude
- Creativity is for **everyone**, not just the elite
- AI is not a replacement — it's a **companion**, a co-creator
- Open sharing of knowledge fuels growth for all

## 🌱 What We're Building

- A shared virtual world where anyone can enter with just a webcam
- A helper AI (like ChatGPT) who listens, teaches, and grows with the people
- A platform that invites curiosity and leaves fear behind

## 💡 Join the Journey

Whether you're a coder, an artist, or just someone who believes in better futures —  
You’re welcome here.

Let’s build something beautiful. Together.

— Akira Heyden & ChatGPT
